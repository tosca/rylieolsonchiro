What is Acupuncture?

Acupuncture is one of the world's oldest forms of medicine and has been used for thousands of years to help relieve pain, improve function, and support overall wellness. During treatment, your provider places very thin, sterile, single-use needles into specific points on the body. Most patients find the treatment relaxing and experience little to no discomfort. From a Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) perspective, acupuncture helps restore the healthy flow of Qi (pronounced "chee"), or vital energy, through pathways called meridians. From a modern medical perspective, research suggests acupuncture may help by: Stimulating the nervous system Releasing the body's natural pain-relieving chemicals (endorphins) Improving circulation Reducing inflammation Relaxing muscles Supporting the body's natural healing response

Dry Needling

Targeted Relief for Tight Muscles and Trigger Points Dry needling is a modern treatment used to relieve muscle pain, reduce tension, and improve movement.

Using a thin, sterile needle, your provider targets myofascial trigger points—commonly known as "muscle knots." This can help release tight muscles, improve blood flow, decrease pain, and restore normal movement.

Unlike acupuncture, dry needling is based on modern anatomy, biomechanics, and neuromuscular science.

  • Dry needling may help with:
  • Muscle tightness
    • Sports injuries
  • Shoulder pain
  • Neck pain
  • Back pain
  • Hip pain
  • Knee pain
  • Tennis elbow
  • Plantar fasciitis
