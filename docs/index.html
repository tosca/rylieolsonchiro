What Is a Chiropractic Adjustment?

Pain. Performance. Prevention.

A chiropractic adjustment is a precise, gentle technique used to improve the movement of joints—most commonly those in the spine. When joints become restricted or don't move as they should, they can contribute to pain, stiffness, muscle tension, and decreased function. Using carefully controlled movements, your chiropractor applies a specific force to a joint to help restore normal motion. The goal is to improve mobility, reduce discomfort, and help your body move and function more efficiently. Every adjustment is customized to your individual needs, comfort level, age, health history, and examination findings.

Why Are Adjustments Performed?

Restricted joint motion can develop from many everyday causes, including:

  • Poor posture
  • Sports injuries
  • Automobile accident
  • Heavy lifting
  • Stress
  • Sitting for long periods
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Repetitive movement

These restrictions may contribute to:

  • Neck pain
  • Back pain
  • Headaches
  • Muscle tightness
  • Joint stiffness
  • Reduced flexibility
  • Decreased athletic performance

Restoring normal joint movement can help reduce these symptoms and improve overall function.

What Happens During an Adjustment?


Your chiropractor will perform a thorough examination before recommending treatment.

  • Gentle hands-on techniques
  • Specialized adjusting instruments
  • Low-force techniques
  • Drop-table (Thompson) techniques
Some patients hear a small "pop" during an adjustment. This sound is simply the release of gas from the joint—similar to cracking your knuckles—and is completely normal.
Not every adjustment produces this sound, and it is not required for treatment to be effective.
