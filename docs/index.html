School Physicals

Helping Students Stay Healthy & Ready to Participate

Whether your child is preparing for sports, marching band, or other school activities, a school physical helps ensure they are healthy and ready to participate safely.
During your visit, our doctor will review your child's medical history, perform a comprehensive physical examination, and complete any required school or athletic forms.

School physicals typically include:

  • Medical history review
  • Height, weight, and BMI measurement
  • Vision and hearing screening
  • Heart and lung examination
  • Musculoskeletal assessment
  • Blood pressure check
  • Vaccination review
  • Posture and movement screening
  • Clearance for sports and school activities

What is a Chiropractic Adjustment?

Pain. Performance. Prevention.

A Chiropractic adjustment is a precise, gentle technique used to improve the movement of joints—most commonly those in the spine. When joints become restricted or don't move as they should, they can contribute to pain, stiffness, muscle tension, and decreased function. Using carefully controlled movements, your chiropractor applies a specific force to a joint to help restore normal motion. The goal is to improve mobility, reduce discomfort, and help your body move and function more efficiently. Every adjustment is customized to your individual needs, comfort level, age, health history, and examination findings.

Personalized care for optimal spinal health.

Why are Adjustments Performed?

  • Poor Posture
  • Repetitive movements
  • Sports injuries
  • Automobile accidents
  • Work-related injuries
  • Heavy lifting
  • Stress
  • Sitting for long periods
  • Normal wear and tear

These restrictions may contribute to:

  • Back pain
  • Neck pain
  • Headaches
  • Shoulder pain
  • Hip pain
  • Knee pain
  • Foot and ankle pain

Acupuncture & TCM

Traditional Chinese Medicine approaches supporting whole-body wellness.

During treatment, your provider places very thin, sterile, single-use needles into specific points on the body. Most patients find the treatment relaxing and experience little to no discomfort.

From a Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) perspective, acupuncture helps restore the healthy flow of Qi (pronounced "chee"), or vital energy, through pathways called meridians.

From a modern medical perspective, research suggests acupuncture may help by:

  • Stimulating the nervous system
  • Releasing the body's natural pain-relieving chemicals (endorphins)
  • Improving circulation
  • Reducing inflammation
  • Relaxing muscles
  • Supporting the body's natural healing response

Dry Needling- Targeted Relief for Tight Muscles and Trigger Points

Dry needling is a modern treatment used to relieve muscle pain, reduce tension, and improve movement. Using a thin, sterile needle, your provider targets myofascial trigger points—commonly known as "muscle knots." This can help release tight muscles, improve blood flow, decrease pain, and restore normal movement.

Unlike acupuncture, dry needling is based on modern anatomy, biomechanics, and neuromuscular science.

  • Muscle tightness
  • Sports injuries
  • Tendenitis
  • Shoulder pain
  • Neck pain
  • Back pain
  • Headaches
  • knee pain
  • Foot and ankle pain
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Tennis elbow

Spinal Decombression

Gentle Relief for Disc and Nerve Conditions

Spinal decompression is a non-surgical therapy designed to gently stretch the spine using a computerized treatment table.

This controlled stretching may help reduce pressure on spinal discs and nerves, allowing nutrients and oxygen to reach injured tissues while encouraging the body's natural healing response. Many patients find spinal decompression relaxing and comfortable.

It may be beneficial for:

  • Herniated discs
  • Bulging discs
  • Degenerative disc disease
  • Sciatica
  • Pinched nerves
  • Chronic neck pain
  • Chronic low back pain

Theraputic Exercise

Building Strength for Long-Term Recovery

Movement is medicine. Therapeutic exercises are customized programs designed to improve flexibility, strength, balance, posture, and overall function. We will prescribe exercises based on your diagnosis, movement patterns, and recovery goals to help reduce pain while preventing future injuries.

Exercises may focus on:

  • Core stability
  • Flexibility
  • Joint mobility
  • Balance
  • Strengthening
  • Postural correction
  • Functional movement
